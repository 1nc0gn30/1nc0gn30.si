01 // LEXICON DEFINITION

    
What is “FieldKit”?

    

      Virtually all frontier models remain confined to chat boxes and IDE tabs that presuppose a desk and two free hands on a keyboard. FieldKit is the paradigm coined by 1nc0gn30 defining how smart glasses, bodycams, and edge sensors act as a proactive, hands-free co-pilot in messy real-world workshops, field diagnostics, and hardware engineering.
    

  


  

    
02 // ARCHITECTURAL RUBRIC

    
The 4 Pillars of FieldKit

    

      

        

          
I. Proactive Anticipation
ANTI-PROMPT

          
Zero Wake-Word Interruption

          
Standard AI waits passively for queries. FieldKit systems observe physical state milestones continuously and speak or project guidance the moment a real-world step completes.

        

      

      

        

          
II. Sub-130ms Grounding
EDGE VISION

          
21-Joint Skeletal Hand Raycasts

          
Replaces slow cloud round trips with tight on-device object bounding boxes and finger ray vectors that confirm physical touch before advancing state.

        

      

      

        

          
III. Wearer-Only Voice Gate
SPECTRAL ISOLATION

          
Near-Field Acoustic Rejection

          
Rejects loud shop machinery, wind, and bystander speech so only the physical wearer can command or interrupt the co-pilot.

        

      

      

        

          
IV. On-Device Biometric Vault
AES-256-GCM

          
Zero-Cloud Spatial Privacy

          
Hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption for facial and skeletal landmark vectors — zero centralized biometric storage or telemetry.

        

      

    

  


  

    
03 // PARADIGM COMPARISON

    
Screen Chat AI vs. FieldKit Physical AI

    

      

        INTERFACE
        Screen AI: Browser tab, phone screen, or desktop IDE
        FieldKit: Smart glasses, helmet optics & edge wearables
      

      

        TRIGGER
        Screen AI: Reactive (waits for human prompt)
        FieldKit: Proactive (tracks physical task milestones)
      

      

        POSTURE
        Screen AI: Seated with both hands occupied typing
        FieldKit: Hands-free on tools, wiring, and diagnostics
