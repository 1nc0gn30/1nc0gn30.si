FieldKit

The sovereign terminology, operational framework, and open standard for bridging artificial intelligence into physical, real-world reality.

Origin: Invented by 1nc0gn30 Entity: NullAI Platform Paradigm: Physical AI (Non-Desktop) Flagship Project: Spotter License: MIT Open Access

1. The Definition: What is "FieldKit"?

In this era of massive, rapid AI developments, virtually all frontier models (LLMs, VLMs, code agents) remain confined to static desktop screens, chat windows, and browser tabs. They presuppose an office desk, two free hands to type, and an unmetered fiber internet connection.

FieldKit is a specialized term and foundational paradigm invented by 1nc0gn30 to give developers a clear roadmap for breaking AI out of the browser and into the physical world.

FieldKit defines how smart glasses, bodycams, edge drones, and handheld sensors must be designed so AI can act as a hands-free, proactive co-pilot in messy physical environments—where hands are greasy or holding tools, ambient noise is loud, and the AI must anticipate the user's next step before they have to stop and ask.

2. Why 1nc0gn30 Coined FieldKit

As frontier multimodal models advance at breakneck speed, developers often fall into "The Passive Earpiece Trap"—wrapping an API call into a mobile app and expecting users to verbally prompt the AI while trying to solder a circuit board or fix an engine.

1nc0gn30 established FieldKit to serve as a beacon and architectural rubric for developers navigating this shift:

I. Proactive Anticipation (The Anti-Prompt)

Standard AI waits for queries. FieldKit-compliant systems observe physical state milestones over time and speak guidance proactively when a physical step is completed.

II. Sub-130ms Spatial Grounding

Bypassing high-latency cloud round trips with tight edge vision bounding boxes and skeletal hand tracking that verify touch before advancing state.

III. Wearer-Only Acoustic Isolation

Near-field vocal gating that rejects loud workshop machinery and bystander chatter, ensuring only the verified wearer can instruct the system.

IV. On-Device Biometric People Vault

Hardware-enclave AES-256-GCM encryption for facial and body landmark vectors. Real-world privacy with zero centralized biometric storage.

3. FieldKit in Action: Spotter

The primary reference implementation of FieldKit is Spotter, created by 1nc0gn30 for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026.

Connecting directly to Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses via the DAT SDK, Spotter watches hands-busy mechanical and electronic tasks, detects physical state milestones via Meta Spark, and guides users hands-free with sub-127ms spatial grounding.
